Cookie Policy – Operlya

Last Updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Operlya uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and session storage) when you visit or use our website and services. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how a site is used.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Operlya groups the cookies and similar technologies it uses into the following categories:

  • Strictly Necessary: required for core functionality such as keeping you signed in (authentication session), remembering security settings, and enabling basic navigation. These cannot be disabled without breaking the Platform, and do not require consent under applicable law.
  • Functional: remember your preferences, such as your selected display language, so you don't have to reconfigure them on every visit.
  • Analytics: help us understand how visitors use Operlya (pages viewed, general navigation patterns) so we can improve the Platform. We currently use Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager for this purpose.
  • Advertising / Measurement: help us measure the performance of our marketing campaigns. We currently use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel for this purpose.

3. Consent

When you first visit Operlya, you are shown a cookie banner where you can accept or reject non-essential cookies (Analytics and Advertising / Measurement categories). Analytics and advertising tags are not loaded until you give your consent. Strictly Necessary and Functional cookies are always active, as the Platform cannot function properly without them. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for operlya.com, which will show the banner again on your next visit.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we rely on, including Google (Analytics / Tag Manager) and Meta (Facebook Pixel), and — for signed-in users — Firebase Authentication for session management. These providers may use the information collected for their own purposes as described in their respective privacy policies. Operlya does not control these third-party cookies directly.

5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

In addition to the choice offered in our cookie banner, most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling Strictly Necessary or Functional cookies may prevent core features — such as staying signed in or saving your language preference — from working correctly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at:
